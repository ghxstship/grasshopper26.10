## Introduction

GVTEWAY is your comprehensive platform for discovering events, purchasing tickets, booking adventures, and connecting with the community. This guide will help you navigate all features and make the most of your experience.

### What You Can Do

- **Discover Events**: Browse upcoming concerts, festivals, sports events, and more
- **Purchase Tickets**: Secure your spot at events with instant digital tickets
- **Book Adventures**: Experience curated activities and tours
- **Shop Marketplace**: Buy event merchandise and exclusive items
- **Connect Socially**: Share experiences, follow artists, and engage with the community
- **Earn Rewards**: Join membership tiers for exclusive benefits

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the GVTEWAY homepage
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top right
3. Enter your email, name, and password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile

### Logging In

1. Click **Log In**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Continue**

### First-Time Setup

After creating your account:
1. **Add Payment Method**: Go to Settings → Payment Methods
2. **Set Preferences**: Choose your favorite event categories
3. **Enable Notifications**: Stay updated on events and offers
4. **Complete Profile**: Add profile picture and bio

## Events

### Browsing Events

1. Navigate to **Events** from the main menu
2. Use filters to narrow down:
   - **Category**: Music, Sports, Arts, Food, Tech
   - **Date Range**: Upcoming, This Week, This Month
   - **Location**: Near you or specific venues
   - **Price Range**: Free, Under $50, Premium

### Event Details

Click any event to view:
- Event description and lineup
- Date, time, and venue information
- Ticket types and pricing
- Venue map and directions
- Reviews and ratings
- Similar events

### Saving Events

- Click the **heart icon** to add events to your wishlist
- Access your wishlist from your profile
- Receive notifications when tickets go on sale

## Tickets

### Purchasing Tickets

1. Select an event
2. Click **Get Tickets**
3. Choose ticket type (General, VIP, etc.)
4. Select quantity
5. Review order and click **Checkout**
6. Complete payment
7. Receive digital tickets via email and app

### Managing Tickets

Access your tickets from **My Tickets**:
- **Active Tickets**: Upcoming events
- **Past Tickets**: Event history
- **Cancelled/Refunded**: Transaction history

### Using Tickets

At the event:
1. Open your ticket in the app
2. Show QR code to staff
3. QR code will be scanned for entry
4. Keep ticket accessible throughout event

### Transferring Tickets

1. Go to **My Tickets**
2. Select the ticket to transfer
3. Click **Transfer**
4. Enter recipient's email
5. Recipient receives transfer notification

### Refunds & Cancellations

- **Refund Policy**: Varies by event (check event page)
- **Request Refund**: Go to ticket → Options → Request Refund
- **Processing Time**: 5-10 business days

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't Log In**
- Verify email and password
- Reset password if needed
- Check for account suspension email
- Contact support if issue persists

**Ticket Not Showing**
- Check email for confirmation
- Refresh My Tickets page
- Check spam folder
- Contact support with order number

**Payment Failed**
- Verify card details
- Check card balance
- Try different payment method
- Contact your bank

**App Not Working**
- Update to latest version
- Clear cache and data
- Restart device
- Reinstall app

### Getting Help

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are tickets refundable?**  
A: Refund policies vary by event. Check the event page for specific terms.

**Q: Can I transfer tickets to someone else?**  
A: Yes, use the Transfer feature in My Tickets.

**Q: How do I cancel my membership?**  
A: Go to Settings → Membership → Cancel Subscription.

**Q: What if I lose my phone with my tickets?**  
A: Log in on another device to access your tickets. They're linked to your account.

**Q: Can I buy tickets for someone else?**  
A: Yes, purchase tickets and transfer them to the recipient.

**Q: How do I contact an event organizer?**  
A: Click "Contact Organizer" on the event page.
